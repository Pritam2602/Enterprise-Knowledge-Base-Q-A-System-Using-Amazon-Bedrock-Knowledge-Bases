================================================================================
                 TECHCORP INDUSTRIES - REMOTE WORK POLICY BOOK
                         People Operations Department
                       Effective Date: May 21, 2026
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy book defines TechCorp Industries' standards for remote, hybrid, and
distributed work. It is intended to help employees work productively while
protecting company data, maintaining team coordination, and preserving customer
service quality.

2. ELIGIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Eligible Roles
Remote work eligibility is based on role requirements, performance history, and
manager approval. Employees in software engineering, design, finance operations,
HR operations, analytics, customer success, and internal IT support may request
remote or hybrid arrangements.

2.2 Ineligible Roles
Roles requiring physical presence, secure facility access, lab equipment,
on-site customer support, or document custody may be ineligible for regular
remote work. Exceptions require approval from the department VP and People
Operations.

2.3 Performance Requirement
Employees must have no active final written warning and must have achieved a
"Meets Expectations" or higher rating in the most recent performance cycle.

3. WORK MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Office-Based
Employees work from a TechCorp office at least 4 days per week.

3.2 Hybrid Standard
Employees work from a TechCorp office 2 or 3 days per week and remotely for the
remaining workdays. Tuesday and Thursday are recommended collaboration days.

3.3 Remote-First
Employees work remotely 4 or 5 days per week. Remote-first status requires
director approval and must be reviewed every 6 months.

3.4 Temporary Remote Work
Employees may request up to 15 temporary remote workdays per calendar year for
personal appointments, home repairs, short-term travel disruption, or family
support needs.

4. CORE HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Core Collaboration Hours
All remote and hybrid employees must be reachable during core collaboration
hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their assigned office time zone, Monday through
Friday, excluding approved leave and public holidays.

4.2 Response Expectations
- Chat messages from direct team members: respond within 2 business hours
- Customer-impacting escalations: respond within 30 minutes during core hours
- Email: respond within 1 business day unless marked urgent
- Calendar invitations: accept, decline, or propose a new time within 1 business day

4.3 Daily Status Signal
Remote employees must keep their calendar and chat status current. Employees
should mark focus time, lunch breaks, school pickup windows, and offline periods
so teams can plan work without unnecessary interruptions.

5. REMOTE WORK SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Approved Devices
Employees must use a company-managed laptop for all company work. Personal
computers, shared family devices, internet cafes, and public kiosk machines are
not approved for company systems.

5.2 Network Requirements
Employees must use a password-protected private network. Public Wi-Fi is allowed
only when connected to the company VPN. Employees must not access confidential
company systems from unsecured networks.

5.3 Screen Privacy
Employees working in public or shared spaces must use a privacy screen when
viewing confidential information. Confidential calls must not be taken where
customer, employee, financial, or product information can be overheard.

5.4 Document Handling
Printed confidential documents are not permitted outside a TechCorp office unless
approved by Legal and Information Security. Any approved printed materials must
be stored in a locked cabinet and shredded after use.

6. EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Standard Equipment Package
Approved hybrid and remote-first employees may request the following equipment:
- One external monitor
- One keyboard and mouse set
- One laptop stand
- One noise-reducing headset
- One webcam if the laptop camera is insufficient

6.2 Home Office Stipend
Remote-first employees are eligible for a one-time home office stipend of $750.
Hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time stipend of $350. Stipends must be
used for ergonomic and productivity-related equipment.

6.3 Internet Reimbursement
Remote-first employees may claim up to $60 per month for home internet service.
Hybrid employees may claim up to $30 per month when they work remotely at least
8 days in that month.

6.4 Non-Reimbursable Costs
TechCorp does not reimburse rent, mortgage payments, utilities, home renovation,
furniture unrelated to work, childcare, pet care, or coworking spaces unless
pre-approved by Finance and the department VP.

7. MEETINGS AND COMMUNICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Camera Guidelines
Cameras are recommended for team meetings, customer meetings, interviews, and
performance conversations. Cameras may be turned off for bandwidth issues,
health reasons, accessibility needs, or large informational meetings.

7.2 Meeting Hygiene
Meeting organizers must include an agenda for meetings longer than 15 minutes.
Recurring meetings should be reviewed quarterly and canceled when they no longer
create clear value.

7.3 Documentation Standard
Important decisions made in remote meetings must be documented in the team
workspace within 1 business day. The note must include the decision, owner,
deadline, and any open risks.

8. LOCATION AND TRAVEL RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Approved Work Location
Employees must work from the country and state listed in HR records unless they
receive written approval before changing work location.

8.2 Working From Another State
Employees may work from another state for up to 20 business days per calendar
year with manager approval. Longer periods require People Operations and Payroll
review due to tax, benefits, and employment law requirements.

8.3 International Remote Work
International remote work is limited to 10 business days per calendar year and
requires approval from Legal, Payroll, Information Security, and the department
VP at least 20 business days before travel.

9. COMPLIANCE AND REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Review Cycle
All remote-first arrangements must be reviewed every 6 months. Hybrid
arrangements must be reviewed annually or when role expectations change.

9.2 Policy Violations
Violations of this policy may result in loss of remote work privileges,
disciplinary action, or termination depending on severity. Security violations
must be reported to Information Security immediately.

9.3 Escalation Contact
Questions about this policy should be sent to remote-work@techcorp.com.
Security-related questions should be sent to security@techcorp.com.
