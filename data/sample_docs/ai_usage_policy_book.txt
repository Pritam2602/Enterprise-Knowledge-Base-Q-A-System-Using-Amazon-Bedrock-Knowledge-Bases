================================================================================
                    TECHCORP INDUSTRIES - AI USAGE POLICY BOOK
                         Information Governance Office
                       Effective Date: May 21, 2026
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines how employees may use artificial intelligence tools,
generative AI assistants, code assistants, summarization tools, transcription
systems, and automated decision-support tools while working for TechCorp
Industries.

2. APPROVED AI TOOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Approved Tool List
Employees may use only AI tools listed in the TechCorp Approved AI Registry.
The registry is maintained by Information Governance and reviewed monthly by
Legal, Security, Procurement, and Enterprise Architecture.

2.2 New Tool Requests
Employees who want to use a new AI tool must submit an AI Tool Review Request
through the Service Desk. Reviews are completed within 10 business days for
standard tools and 20 business days for tools that process confidential data.

2.3 Personal AI Accounts
Employees must not use personal AI accounts for company work. This includes
personal chatbot accounts, browser extensions, personal code assistants, and
consumer transcription tools.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Public Information
Public information may be entered into approved AI tools when the output is
reviewed by an employee before publication or customer use.

3.2 Internal Information
Internal information may be entered only into approved enterprise AI tools that
have a signed vendor agreement and security review.

3.3 Confidential Information
Confidential information may be entered only into approved restricted AI tools.
Examples include customer contracts, employee records, unreleased financial
results, source code from private repositories, security findings, and roadmap
materials.

3.4 Restricted Information
Restricted information must not be entered into any AI tool unless Legal and
Information Security grant written exception approval. Restricted information
includes authentication secrets, private keys, production credentials, payment
card data, health information, government identifiers, and merger documents.

4. HUMAN REVIEW REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 No Fully Automated Decisions
AI tools must not make final employment, compensation, disciplinary, credit,
legal, vendor selection, or customer-impacting decisions without human review.

4.2 Required Verification
Employees must verify AI-generated facts, calculations, policy interpretations,
legal language, and technical recommendations before relying on them.

4.3 Customer-Facing Content
AI-generated customer-facing content must be reviewed by the responsible content
owner. Legal review is required for contractual language, claims about product
capabilities, regulatory statements, and financial representations.

5. SOFTWARE ENGINEERING USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Code Assistance
Developers may use approved code assistants for boilerplate, tests, refactoring
suggestions, documentation drafts, and debugging support.

5.2 Prohibited Code Inputs
Developers must not paste production secrets, private customer data, incident
logs containing credentials, or unreleased security vulnerabilities into AI
tools.

5.3 Required Code Review
All AI-assisted code must pass normal peer review, automated testing, security
scanning, and license compliance checks before merge.

5.4 Open Source Risk
If an AI tool produces code that appears copied from a public project, the
developer must consult Open Source Compliance before using it.

6. MEETING TRANSCRIPTION AND SUMMARIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Consent Requirement
AI transcription tools may be used only when meeting participants are notified
at the start of the meeting. External meetings require explicit customer or
partner consent before recording or transcription begins.

6.2 Restricted Meetings
AI transcription is not allowed in meetings involving layoffs, employee health
matters, legal strategy, acquisition planning, board discussions, or active
security incidents unless Legal approves an exception.

6.3 Retention
AI-generated meeting summaries must be stored in the approved team workspace.
Raw transcripts must be deleted within 30 days unless Legal Hold applies.

7. MODEL OUTPUT LABELING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Internal Labeling
Documents substantially drafted with AI must include the note "AI-assisted
draft reviewed by owner" in the internal metadata or document footer.

7.2 External Labeling
External disclosure is required when AI-generated content could reasonably be
mistaken for human-authored expert advice, original research, legal advice, or
financial guidance.

8. INCIDENT REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Reportable Incidents
Employees must report the following incidents to security@techcorp.com within
24 hours:
- Confidential or restricted data entered into an unapproved AI tool
- AI tool account compromise
- AI-generated output sent to a customer with material errors
- Suspected leakage of source code, credentials, or customer data
- Discovery that an AI tool stores data outside approved regions

8.2 Response
Information Security will assess the incident, coordinate containment, notify
Legal when needed, and determine whether vendor or customer notification is
required.

9. TRAINING AND COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Required Training
All employees must complete AI Acceptable Use Training every 12 months. Employees
using restricted AI tools must complete an additional 30-minute secure prompting
module before access is granted.

9.2 Access Review
Access to restricted AI tools is reviewed quarterly. Employees who no longer
need access will have permissions removed.

9.3 Violations
Violations of this policy may result in removal of AI tool access, disciplinary
action, termination, or legal action depending on severity.

10. POLICY CONTACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
General AI policy questions should be sent to ai-governance@techcorp.com.
Security questions should be sent to security@techcorp.com.
Legal review requests should be sent to legal-ops@techcorp.com.
