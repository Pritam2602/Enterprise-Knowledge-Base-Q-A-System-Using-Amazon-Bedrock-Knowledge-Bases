================================================================================
              TECHCORP INDUSTRIES - DATA RETENTION POLICY
                    Legal and Compliance Department
                      Last Updated: March 10, 2026
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines how TechCorp Industries stores, retains, archives, and
disposes of company records and business data. The goal is to preserve required
information, reduce unnecessary storage of outdated data, and ensure records are
available for audits, legal reviews, and business continuity needs.

2. SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third
parties who create, receive, store, process, or dispose of TechCorp data.

Covered systems include:
- Corporate email and collaboration tools
- Customer relationship management (CRM) systems
- Finance, payroll, and procurement platforms
- Product analytics and operational logs
- Shared drives and document repositories
- Backup, archive, and disaster recovery systems

3. DATA RETENTION CATEGORIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Corporate Records
Corporate governance records must be retained permanently. These records include:
- Board meeting minutes
- Articles of incorporation and company bylaws
- Stock and ownership records
- Major corporate transaction approvals
- Annual audited financial statements

3.2 Financial and Tax Records
Financial and tax records must be retained for 7 years from the end of the
fiscal year in which the record was created.

Examples include:
- General ledger entries
- Accounts payable and receivable records
- Tax filings and supporting documentation
- Vendor invoices
- Purchase orders and payment approvals

3.3 Employee Records
Employee personnel files must be retained for 6 years after employment ends.
Payroll records must be retained for 7 years after the payroll date.

Employee records include:
- Offer letters and employment agreements
- Performance reviews
- Promotion and compensation history
- Disciplinary notices
- Termination documentation

3.4 Customer Contract Records
Customer contracts, order forms, statements of work, and contract amendments must
be retained for 8 years after contract expiration or termination.

Customer support tickets related to contractual obligations must be retained for
3 years after ticket closure.

3.5 Product and Engineering Records
Product requirements, architecture decisions, and release approval records must
be retained for 5 years after the related product version is retired.

Source code repositories are retained for the active life of the product plus
10 years after product retirement.

3.6 System Logs and Monitoring Data
Operational logs must be retained based on business and security need:
- Standard application logs: 180 days
- Security event logs: 2 years
- Production access logs: 1 year
- Debug logs containing customer data: 30 days maximum

Debug logs must be reviewed and deleted earlier if they contain sensitive data
that is no longer required for troubleshooting.

4. LEGAL HOLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A legal hold overrides all normal retention and deletion schedules. When a legal
hold is issued, employees must preserve all relevant records until the Legal
Department releases the hold in writing.

Employees must not delete, alter, archive, overwrite, or dispose of records that
are subject to a legal hold. This requirement applies even if the record has
reached the end of its normal retention period.

Questions about legal holds must be sent to legalhold@techcorp.com.

5. ARCHIVING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Records retained for more than 3 years should be moved to an approved archive
system when they are no longer needed for daily operations.

Approved archive locations include:
- TechCorp Records Vault
- Finance Archive Repository
- HR Secure Archive
- Approved cloud storage buckets managed by IT

Archive systems must provide:
- Access controls based on business need
- Encryption at rest
- Audit logs for access and deletion activity
- Searchable metadata for record owner, record type, and retention date

6. DATA DISPOSAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Records must be securely disposed of when their retention period expires and no
legal hold, audit request, or business exception applies.

Disposal methods:
- Paper records: Cross-cut shredding or certified secure destruction
- Electronic files: Secure deletion using IT-approved tools
- Storage media: Cryptographic erase or physical destruction
- Cloud records: Deletion through approved lifecycle management controls

Departments must keep disposal certificates or deletion audit logs for 2 years.

7. EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the record owner, the
Legal Department, and Information Security.

Exception requests must include:
- Business reason for the exception
- Record types affected
- Requested retention period
- Security and privacy impact
- Proposed review date

Approved exceptions must be reviewed at least once every 12 months.

8. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees:
- Classify and store records in approved systems
- Follow retention and disposal instructions
- Preserve records subject to legal holds

Managers:
- Ensure team records are stored in appropriate repositories
- Review access to archived records twice per year
- Confirm disposal activities during quarterly compliance reviews

Legal Department:
- Issue and release legal holds
- Interpret retention requirements
- Approve policy exceptions

Information Security:
- Validate secure storage and disposal methods
- Monitor archive access logs
- Approve tools used for electronic deletion

Records Management Team:
- Maintain the retention schedule
- Coordinate archive transfers
- Provide guidance on record classification

9. REVIEW AND COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Records Management Team reviews this policy annually. Departments may be
audited at any time for compliance with retention, archiving, and disposal
requirements.

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, loss of
system access, or referral to Legal for further review.

For questions, contact the Records Management Team at records@techcorp.com or
ext. 2460.
